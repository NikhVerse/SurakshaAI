# SurakshaAI — Barrier Intelligence

## 1. The Central Paradigm Shift

Traditional safety metrics count lagging injuries: *How many workers were hurt this quarter?*

Barrier intelligence asks the forward-looking operational question:
> **Which critical control was supposed to prevent the hazardous energy pathway, and what happened to that control?**

In major hazard industries, safety is maintained by barriers. When barriers are repeatedly bypassed, unverified, or degraded without consequence, an organization experiences *drift toward failure*.

---

## 2. Barrier Classification

SurakshaAI maps controls across 8 operational barrier categories:
1. **Energy Isolation**: Lockout/Tagout, blind flanging, visual air-gap disconnects.
2. **Work Authorisation**: Permit to work (PTW), job safety analysis (JSA), tool box talks.
3. **Gas Testing**: Pre-entry tests, continuous multi-gas monitors, fixed infrared detectors.
4. **Guarding / Interlock**: Conveyor nip guards, safety gates, limit switches.
5. **Fall Protection**: Engineered anchor lifelines, harnesses, inertial reels.
6. **Exclusion Zone**: Crane swing radius barriers, high-pressure test perimeters.
7. **Lifting Control**: Rigging certification, lift plans, designated spotters.
8. **Traffic Control**: Site speed governors, segregation berms, pedestrian crossings.

---

## 3. Barrier Health States

- **Verified (Normal)**: Control inspected and documented.
- **Degraded**: Control present but operating with diminished capacity or wear.
- **Bypassed**: Control intentionally defeated to expedite work.
- **Unverified**: Work proceeded assuming barrier was active without testing for dead.
- **Absent**: Mandatory barrier omitted completely.
