# SurakshaAI — Product Overview

## 1. Executive Summary

**SurakshaAI** is an explainable industrial safety critical-risk intelligence decision-support platform designed for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) organizations operating in high-hazard industrial environments (oil and gas, petrochemicals, heavy manufacturing, and energy).

The platform bridges the critical operational gap between unstructured safety incident narratives (unsafe acts, unsafe conditions, near misses, and hazard observations) and actionable executive risk priorities:

> **Narrative → Safety Context → Hazard → Hazardous Energy → Exposure → Critical Barrier → Barrier State → Deviation/Failure → Credible Consequence → pSIF Potential → Life-Saving Rule → Similar Cases → Recurring Precursors → Trends → Evidence Package → HSE Review**

---

## 2. Non-Negotiable Boundaries

To maintain rigorous industrial credibility and safety ethics:
- **SurakshaAI is NOT a chatbot.**
- **SurakshaAI is NOT a generic incident classifier.**
- **SurakshaAI is NOT an accident predictor.**
- **SurakshaAI is an explainable AI-driven critical-risk intelligence decision-support system.**

The platform communicates uncertainty with complete scientific honesty. Final safety interpretation, root-cause investigation, and corrective actions remain strictly within the purview of qualified human HSE professionals.

---

## 3. The Five Core Questions

Every operational screen in SurakshaAI is designed to answer five fundamental questions for an HSE manager or analyst:
1. **What happened?** (Factual, normalized event narrative with highlighted safety evidence spans)
2. **Why could it matter?** (Calibrated pSIF probability, Suraksha Priority Score, and credible consequence pathway)
3. **Which barrier was affected?** (Identified critical barrier, observed barrier state, and deviation mode)
4. **Is it happening elsewhere?** (Recurring precursor clusters, semantically similar historical cases across sites)
5. **What should HSE look at first?** (Triage prioritization based on barrier criticality and recurrence rather than raw injury counts)

---

## 4. Key Differentiators

- **Decoupled Risk ML & Private LLM**: The LLM is strictly not the risk predictor. Calibrated gradient-boosted models predict pSIF probabilities, while private local LLMs (Ollama) synthesize grounded human-readable explanations.
- **Barrier Intelligence**: Moves beyond lagging injury metrics to evaluate whether critical controls were *Verified, Failed, Degraded, Bypassed, Absent, or Unverified*.
- **Prompt Injection Defense**: Every safety report narrative and uploaded PDF is treated as untrusted data with strict boundaries.
- **Source-Aware RAG**: Knowledge documents cite specific standards, clauses, and authorities (e.g. IOGP, Corporate HSE Standards).
